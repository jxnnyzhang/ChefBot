About ChefBot

ChefBot is a recipe-finding chatbot that helps you figure out what to cook with what you already have. Tell it a few ingredients from your fridge or pantry and it matches them against a curated collection of popular recipes — typos and all, thanks to fuzzy matching.

ChefBot runs entirely in your browser: no server, no accounts, no tracking. Just say hi, ask what it can do, or list your ingredients to get started.

Experience the joy of cooking with ChefBot and elevate your home cooking to new heights. Try ChefBot today and embark on a culinary adventure like no other.

ChefBot is an ongoing project and is continuously being improved. Its recipe suggestions come from a small hand-picked dataset, not a live database, so it won't know every dish — but it'll do its best with what you give it. Enjoy cooking with ChefBot!
